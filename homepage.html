ABOUT OUR BAKERY

Made with care. Made to share. 🥖

SG Bakery started with one simple idea: make bakery favourites that taste homemade.

Every morning, our bakers mix, shape and bake fresh treats so you can enjoy that just-baked feeling.

From breakfast favourites and Vietnamese signatures to cakes, finger food and drinks, there is something for every occasion.
